# Basic example: discovery-question-architect

## Input
Topic: Why mid-market customers downgrade after their renewal. Length: 45 min.

## Expected output (abbreviated)
**Bank** (excerpt):
- Context: `Walk me through the week leading up to your renewal decision.`
- Pain: `What was the hardest part about your last 6 months on our product?`
- Workaround: `When [X] didn''t work the way you needed, what did you do instead?`

**Bias audit**:
| Original | Score | Rewrite |
|---|---|---|
| Was the price increase frustrating for you? | 3 (leading) | How did you react when you saw the renewal pricing? |
| Why did you downgrade and what would have changed your mind? | 2 (double) | Split into 2 questions. |
